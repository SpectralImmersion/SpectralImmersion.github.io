Understanding Your Colorado Springs Utilities Electric Charges

  • Access Charges: These recover the costs related to electric lines, power plants, metering and billing systems. You pay a fixed daily fee, plus a per-kWh base charge based on usage.
  • Electric Cost Adjustment (ECA): A periodic rate adjustment that covers the cost of fuel used to generate electricity and purchase renewable energy.
  • Electric Capacity Charge (ECC): Covers the costs of delivering natural gas used for electric generation and grid delivery maintenance.
