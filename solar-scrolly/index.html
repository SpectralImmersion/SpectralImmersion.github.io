    From Planetary Orbit to Axial Tilt & The Sky Dome

    In heliocentric space, Earth glides along an ellipse governed by Kepler’s laws. But we live on a tilted, rotating platform. Earth’s rotational axis is tilted by 23.44° relative to its orbital plane.

    This constant tilt produces the seasons and causes the Sun to oscillate between the northern and southern skies. Below, the heliocentric orrery is wholly replaced by the geocentric declination view and the 3D topocentric celestial sphere.
