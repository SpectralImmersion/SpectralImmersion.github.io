    Points, Lines & Polylines

    At the foundation of svg3d.js, a pinhole perspective camera projects any 3D coordinate P=(x,y,z)\mathbf{P} = (x, y, z)P=(x,y,z) into 2D viewport coordinates (u,v)(u, v)(u,v) scaled by focal length f=H/2tan⁡(FOV/2)f = \frac{H/2}{\tan(\text{FOV}/2)}f=tan(FOV/2)H/2​:

    u=W2+xcamzcamf,v=H2−ycamzcamfu = \frac{W}{2} + \frac{x_{\text{cam}}}{z_{\text{cam}}} f, \qquad v = \frac{H}{2} - \frac{y_{\text{cam}}}{z_{\text{cam}}} fu=2W​+zcam​xcam​​f,v=2H​−zcam​ycam​​f

    By chaining projected points with cam.line(p1, p2) and cam.parametric(fn), we can trace continuous trajectories in R3\mathbb{R}^3R3 with front/rear depth styling.
