    Beyond Physical Space: The Geometry of Phase Space

    Watching a physical mass bounce back and forth gives us intuitive mechanical feedback, but it hides the underlying geometric conservation laws governing harmonic motion. A physical state is not just where an object is—it is also where it is going.

    By constructing an orthogonal plane with displacement (xxx) on the horizontal axis and velocity (vvv) on the vertical axis, the 1D bounce is transformed into a continuous, uniform circular orbit.

    Below, the physical spring graphic is wholly replaced by this new state-space portrait to explore how friction and damping reshape the geometry of motion.
